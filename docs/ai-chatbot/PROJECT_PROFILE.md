# ResolveIQ AI project profile

Goal: reduce time-to-triage and institutional knowledge loss in financial operations without delegating operational authority to AI.

Users: reporter, responder, incident manager, knowledge curator, tenant admin, platform admin. The AI assists with category/severity proposals, missing information, evidence-backed hypotheses and reversible actions. It cannot change incident state, execute actions or publish knowledge.

Success measures: faster acknowledgement/triage, cited evidence precision, action acceptance and success, knowledge reuse, no-answer correctness, tenant leakage rate (target zero), p95 latency and safe fallback rate.
